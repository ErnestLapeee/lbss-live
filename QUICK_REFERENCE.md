## 🎯 MOST IMPORTANT STATS

### Batting Essentials
- **Traditional:** G, PA, AB, R, H, 2B, 3B, HR, RBI, AVG, BB, SO, SB
- **Advanced:** OBP, SLG, OPS, BABIP, wRC
- **Clutch:** BA/RSP, H/RSP, RBI
- **Quality:** QAB1, QAB2, QAB3

### Pitching Essentials
- **Traditional:** G, W, L, SV, IP, ERA, K, BB, H, WHIP
- **Advanced:** FIP, BAA, BABIP, K/BB, K/9
- **Effectiveness:** FPS%, GO/AO, 123 innings
- **Quality:** GSc, QS, TL, CW

### Fielding Essentials
- **Overall:** G, PO, A, E, FP%, DP
- **Position-specific:** FP1-FP10 (fielding % by position)
- **Catcher:** SBA, CS, PB, PK
